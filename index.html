Before enabling online AI — what this means

Switching to Online mode sends your chat messages to an external AI service operated by Mistral AI (mistral.ai). Nothing leaves your device until you also give AI consent.

AI model: mistral-small-latest
Data sent to Mistral: The full text of each message you send while AI is enabled.
Who receives it: Mistral AI, a French company subject to EU law.
Data retention: Mistral's API terms state that messages sent via the API are not used to train models by default. See mistral.ai/privacy.
Training use: Not used for training under the standard API agreement.
Your control: You can switch back to Offline mode or type /consent no at any time to stop AI processing.

Online mode alone does not send data. Data is only sent when you also give AI consent (by typing /consent yes or clicking "Yes, I consent" in the AI dialog).
